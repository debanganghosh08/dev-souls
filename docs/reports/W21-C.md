# W21-C: the map as a map
No Godot, suite or git run by me (per the brief); the lead ran the suite once, results below. `main.gd` untouched.
## What it draws now
- **Relief** (`scripts/ui/map_relief.gd`, new): a 256 px `Image` sampled from `Heightfield.from_dict(area)`, the terrain's own
  object: hypsometric tint, NW hillshade from finite-difference normals, contours every 8 m (heavy every 40 m), water from
  the pond basin and `Heightfield.in_river`, alpha fade past `ridge.outer_radius` so the Vale reads as one landmass.
- **Water** (`map_shapes.gd`, new): river as quads plus joint discs, widths from `vale.json` `waters` (9 m, 15 m at the Wide
  Water, rim taper), a 2 m shore rim then the fill; pond disc with a hairline shore. **Roads**: valley waypoints as a cased
  line; other `material.road` segments as dashed tracks, consecutive-pair as `terrain_material.gd` feeds the shader (lead:
  that gives two straight tracks across the Vale; it is the data). **Names** (`map_labels.gd`, new): regions large, faint,
  uppercase, behind all; places by kind (castle/village 15 px, tower/ruin/chapel 12, croft 11, woods/water unmarked); reaches
  in blue; haloed ink. **Player**: outlined arrow, ring, 70 deg wedge. `map_view.gd` rewritten (287 lines) to draw world
  geometry under one `draw_set_transform`; `map_draw.gd` gains `heading()`.

## Caching
One `MapRelief` per area name, kept across `bind_level`'s second `load_world`; both views hold the handle and draw it with one
`draw_texture_rect`. The build is sliced: `_process` calls `step(3000 usec)` per frame until done (~3 ms a frame at most);
`open()` before that calls `build_all()` once (one hitch, UNMEASURED); until then both views draw the old flat ring. Shapes, site
colours and label layout are cached per world/size; `refresh()` redraws only on movement; the minimap only ever draws the texture.

## Label collisions
Rank order (castles first); a label tries right, left, near above/below, then far above/below (clearing a side label), and takes
the first slot inside the map that hits no placed box (2 px pad); one that fits nowhere is not written, its marker still is.

  Expectation kept: four written, two dropped, none overlapping [ignoring collisions places all six].
