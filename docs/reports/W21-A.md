## Faults and their mechanisms
1. **Deck under water.** Crossings defaulted to `ground:"max"` over a 32 m footprint lying wholly inside a 74 m cut, so "max" was riverbed. Fix:
   `ground:"water"` in `Settlements.ground_height` = `max(hf.surface_at + WATER_CLEARANCE (2 m), footprint max)`: neither in the water nor under a bank.
2. **No way onto the deck.** Nothing joined deck to bank, and the bank is a different height at every crossing (gorge to +18 m, or barely above the
   water), so no fixed ramp can be written in a layout. Fix: a `stairs` entry with `"reach": "ground"`: `StructureBuilder._spawn_flight` walks a flight
   down from the deck end (0.25 risers, under the 0.4 step-up) until the next tread would be under terrain; built per placement, never shared.
   `Settlements.builder_for` feeds it `hf.height_at` and the site transform. `StructureKit.stairs` gained an optional `skirt` (default 0).
3. **Piers.** Three piers (z −10, 0, 10; 26 m tall) plus 30 m abutment blocks (24 m) under each causeway, all reaching under any bed. `river_bridge`
   is 90 m (the cut is 74–90 m across; the `_comment`'s "36 m" was wrong, the deck was 30 m). `hanging_bridge`: 80 m between anchors at ±40, flights off them.
4. **Water not rendering.** (a) The sheet was `half_width` 9 while the ground meets the surface 18–31 m out in the 28 m bank: 10–20 m of bare bed
   each side. (b) `river_wide` drew a second sheet on exactly the plane of the first over the whole middle reach: z-fighting at the hanging and
   Riverend crossings. No geometric gap in `walk()`/`ribbon()`; `_levels_along` blended round the nearest POINT, a small step where that point changed.
   Fix: `spread` (= the cut's bank) per water entry, sheet half = `half_width + spread` (the map still reads `half_width`); a reach sharing all its
   points with a longer water folds into it (`Water.fold_reaches`): one sheet, a width per point; `_levels_along` projects onto the nearest segment.
5. **Reads like the pond**: a sheet as wide as the whole cut, hidden wherever the bank is higher. `cuts` untouched; bed and surface stay one list (D-040).
   Bank not narrowed: `test_w10_village.gd` (not mine) pins bank slope < 0.85 m/m against the 18.7 m gorge, so bank ≥ 22 m; 28 → 24 gains little and
   risks a test I cannot run. Kill volume: as wide as the sheet, top `WADE_DEPTH` 0.5 m BELOW the surface (was 0.4 above): knee-deep kills, a bank does not.
