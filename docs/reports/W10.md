# W10: Hollowmere, and the people in it

Five blocks the owner asked for in one pass, on a weekly budget with 7-8% left. Order was chosen by value
per token spent, and the whole thing came to four full test runs.

## What went in

**1. The village merged.** Antigravity built it as data under the no-code rule and it held: 60 sites, 15 new
building layouts, 57 fires, zero program files touched, 769 piece instances against a 1,200 budget. Reviewed
before merging (see the branch `antigravity/w10-village`): valid kinds and materials throughout, correct
wall rotations, an upper-floor door aligned to its stair landing. Its litter (`out.txt`, `err.txt`) was
removed; the report and requests file it owed were never written, so this report stands in for both.

**2. The village filled.** 4 things to find (Smith's Tongs, Miller's Measure, Lamplighter's Wick, Ox-bow --
new items, because every item in this game has exactly one source and the suite catches a second one) and 2
lore stones. Items 38 -> 42.

**3. The forest band.** `Forest.plan_band` plants trees along a polyline rather than round a centre: a point
on the line, pushed out sideways between `band_gap` and `band_half`, biased toward the road so it crowds
near the track and thins outward. The Vale carries one down the castle road, 7.5 m clear either side out to
30 m, 240 trees asked for, plus **one tree at 3.4x scale** as the landmark.

**4. People and animals.** 10 villagers and 13 animals, all props on sites that already existed. A villager
is the `Creature` wander AI, the player's body, and a `LoreStone` on a child area; its line comes from the
site file through a new child-path form in `set_coerced` (`"Talk/lore_key"`). Ten written lines, one each.

**5. Rest fires.** 6 of them. Press E and the clock moves to the next of dawn, noon, dusk, midnight, and
the whole world follows because everything already reads `Game.time_of_day`.

## Checks

- Suite **434 -> 441, 0 failures**, 0 SCRIPT ERROR in both streams.
- Live: `PROBE sites fixed=3 spawned=151 props=69 pieces=1354`, `PROBE fires total=164 drawing=14`,
  `PROBE forest trees=213 on_ground=true`, grass worst fill frame **5.55 ms** (p95 4.1).
- Four failures were found and fixed on the way, all mine: four village pickups borrowed items that already
  had a source elsewhere; a rest-stop assertion that was wrong rather than the code; a pinned tool list; and
  a grass tail that grew from 8 to 11 pending cells when 60 sites enlarged the keep-out mask (limit raised
  to 14, with the player-facing assertion -- nothing unfilled within 50 m of a sprinting player -- unchanged
  and still passing at 0).

## Not verified, plainly

- **None of it has been seen.** Not one villager, not one tree of the band, not the village at night.
- **`PROBE forest trees=213` is the whole forest after filtering**, radial and band together. I could not
  separate the band's trees from the rest without another run, so *that the band is dense enough to read as
  a wood* is unverified. `test_the_vale_actually_carries_a_forest_band` proves the level asks for one; only
  a recording proves it arrived.
- **Villagers wear the player's body and face.** They differ by nothing but position. No faces, no voices --
  voices need audio files, which cannot go in the repo and are blocked on the Freesound key (O-2).
- **44 fires can fall in one cull sphere** in Hollowmere, against a budget of 45. At the limit.
- **Water and boats were not attempted** (W10-W). It is a system, not content, and it is the one item on the
  owner's list that cannot be done as data.
