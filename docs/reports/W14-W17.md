# W14–W17: water, the river, hills, and light indoors

Four pieces of work done overnight on 30–31 October, each committed and pushed separately. The owner asked
for water, a river, mountains, bridges, a riverside village, a church, a flood pool, more rock, fuller
empty areas and better graphics. What follows is what arrived and what did not.

## W14 — water, and the river

**There was no water in this game at all.** The Mirror Pond had a flat transparent plane over it and
nothing else; no river, no shader, no volume, no rule.

- **`Water`** (`scripts/systems/water.gd`) builds a surface from a polyline — a ribbon of quads at a fixed
  level — or a disc for a pool, plus a **volume of killing water** under it. Touching the river drowns you
  over 1.6 s, which is the owner's rule: *"if you cross the river the character will die"*. Not instant,
  which would read as a bug.
- **`water.gdshader`**: two crossed scrolling noise fields for the ripple and the normal, a depth fade from
  shallow green to deep, and an edge fade where it meets the bank. No texture, so it can never be a missing
  asset (D-005). **It did not compile the first time** — Godot 4 removed the built-in `DEPTH_TEXTURE` in
  favour of a uniform with a hint, and the only sign was one line in a child process's stderr.
- **A negative crest carves.** The same feature that raises a hill cuts a channel when its height is below
  zero, so the river bed and the hills are one mechanism.
- **The river is hand-routed, and D-032 explains why at length.** A greedy router was tried, then
  relaxation sweeps, then naming the chronic offenders as obstacles, then moving the affected sites off the
  bank. **Every version found a new set of buildings to tilt the ground out from under**, because this
  valley was laid out over four weeks before it had a river and 196 sites do not move for one. Eight
  hand-picked waypoints across the empty north work: 120 m clear of Castle Morrow, north of the old road,
  reaching the rim at both ends.
- **Crossings are exempt** from the dry-ground and foundation-cover rules. A bridge stands over a gap: that
  is what makes it a bridge. Three of them, one a hanging bridge on masts and iron stays.
- Also placed: a **village of eight on the bank**, a **chapel** with a bell and a lit altar, the **flood
  pool**, the **crag castle**, the **road cut**, **three war camps** (empty, as asked) and the W12 layouts
  that had never found ground.

## W15 — the pond

The pond's plane was flat transparent glass, which is why it read as a grey bowl rather than as water. It
wears the river's shader now. My new node had also taken the unique name `%Water`, which the pond plane
already owned — a parse error that only appeared inside a child process. Rivers are `%Rivers`.

`Water` learned to drain: a pool names the flag that empties it, and draining removes the surface and the
drowning together. A test caught the obvious thing — a pool is one centre and a radius, and the builder
demanded two points, so every pool silently built nothing.

## W16 — hills, and global illumination

**Two hills, 13 m and 12 m**, on the only two pieces of ground with 81 m and 76 m clear, with a ruined
watchtower on each head. **They are hills, not the mountains that were asked for.** A crest tilts ground
far past its own edge, the whole skirt has to clear every site, and this valley has no room for a mountain.
Saying so is cheaper than pretending otherwise.

**SDFGI is on** — real bounced light instead of a flat ambient term. It is the most expensive thing in this
environment and the most likely to misbehave on geometry built from thin boxes. **Light leaking through a
wall is the failure to watch for**, and it is one line to revert.

## W17 — the empty rim, and light indoors

- **26 sites in the outer rim**, the band between the last buildings and the ridge wall, empty since week
  one. Crags, boulder fields, standing stones, graveyards, blade monuments, war camps.
- **A fire inside 18 houses.** SDFGI bounces light but there has to be light in the room to bounce. Each
  burns at 95 % even at noon, because a shuttered stone room is dark at noon. Six outdoor lamps beside the
  same doors were retired: the light moved indoors rather than being added to the pile.
- Night ambient lifted across 31 keyframes, so an unlit corner is dark rather than absolutely black.

## Where it stands

- **225 sites, 1,899 structure pieces, 272 fires (24 drawing), 621 trees, 28,522 grass blades.**
- **Suite 444 → 446, 0 failures, 0 script errors.** Build exported and verified after every commit.

## Not done, and not claimed

- **Mountains.** Hills of 12–13 m is what the ground allowed. Real mountains need an area this valley does
  not have — an argument for a second area, not for squeezing harder.
- **Wyrdhollow is a hamlet**, six buildings. Its valley wall is a ring 32 m across and a town needs more.
- **Enemies** in the war camps — the owner asked to leave them empty for now.
- **NPC faces and voices.** One character model; villagers differ by clothes and position. Voices need
  audio files, which cannot be committed and are blocked on an API key.

## The thing to do next

**Record it.** Everything above was built against a null renderer. The pond's new shader, the river, the
drowning, SDFGI and eighteen lit interiors have never been seen by anybody. Two recordings have already
overturned six things the tests called fine, and the two most likely failures here — SDFGI leaking through
walls, and the water surface reading wrong where it meets the bank — are both things a thirty-second clip
settles and no test can.
