# W9-G / W9-T / W9-C: what the recording changed

The owner sent 83 seconds of gameplay (`game_recordings/current_gameplay.mp4`, git-ignored with the rest of
the heavy files). Fourteen frames were pulled from it at 6-second intervals and read. It is the first time
anyone has seen this world, and it is worth saying plainly what it showed, because four of these had been
"UNVERIFIED by eye" in this repo for weeks and all four were wrong:

- **The grass read as desert agave.** Metre-wide splayed fans standing about a metre apart, pale straw, over
  bare brown ground. Nothing in the headless measurements said so: density was 1.15 clumps/m², which sounds
  like grass until you see that each clump is a 1 m star and the ground between them is dirt.
- **The fog was a wall at roughly 60 m.** In frame after frame the houses are grey smudges and the castle is
  not there at all. One frame shows a fire at ~40 m, and it reads beautifully — which is the point: W9-F put
  103 fires in the valley and the fog was hiding nearly all of them.
- **There is no road**, exactly as the owner said. The grass thins along the valley line, but thin grass over
  the same ground texture is not a road.
- **No fences, no gardens, nothing around a house but grass.**

## What changed

**Grass and ground (W9-G).**
- `data/grass/vale.json`: base grass **1.15 -> 2.8 clumps/m²**, height **0.28-0.62 -> 0.15-0.33 m**, blade
  width 0.05 -> 0.035, blades 4 -> 3, `align` **0.6 -> 0.22** (the tilt was what made them splay like aloe),
  `dry_amount` 0.45 -> 0.2, `patch_min` 0.5 -> 0.78 (the owner asked for consistent, and patchiness was
  half the unevenness), and the palette from straw (`#9a9a52`) to green (`#7f9340`). Tall grass dropped from
  0.8-1.2 m to 0.55-0.95 m and thinned in width, since those were the worst offenders by eye.
- `data/grass/zones_vale.json`: nothing is bald any more. Hilltops and ridge foot **0.6 -> 0.85x**, village
  and castle interiors **0.45 -> 0.7x**, the road 0.04 -> 0.12x, and the whole valley's palette moved green
  together so the dry places still read as drier.
- **`grass_tint` in the terrain shader**, applied to the grass layer only: density alone could not fix the
  ground BETWEEN the blades, which is a dry aerial-grass photo and reads as dirt. The Vale passes
  `[0.63, 0.82, 0.47]`.
- **Fog cut to a third** across all 31 keyframes (0.034-0.050 -> 0.012-0.017).
- **The old road is worn into the terrain shader**: up to eight segments of the same valley polyline the
  grass zones and the placement rules already use, gravel blended over grass within 2.6 m and fading over
  3.4 m, and fading out as the slope turns to rock because a cart never wore a path up a cliff.
- **Every house and the village got a yard**: a fence run or two (`fence`, already in the kit) and a kitchen
  garden (`garden_bed`, new: a low mound of turned earth with rows of sprouts). Neither is a `Footprint`
  ground kind, so no house moved on its ground.

**The tall towers (W9-T).** Three watchtowers and a light tower, generated by a script rather than
hand-written, from existing kit kinds only:
- **watchtower** 5 storeys, **18.0 m (59 ft)**, 42 pieces; **watchtower_ruined** 4 storeys, 14.6 m (48 ft);
  **lighthouse** 4 storeys to a walk-around terrace and a lantern room, 16.5 m (54 ft).
- A storey is four walls, one straight flight turning 180° from the one below, a floor slab that stops short
  of the stairwell, and a landing at the head. The top two storeys of a watchtower have lost their faces, a
  beacon burns at every head (visible 240-420 m at night), and the ground storey has a door with a doorstep.
- **A new islet in the Mirror Pond** carries the light tower: a plateau 2.6 m clear of the water, set 34 m
  out on the perpendicular of the old road so it crowds neither the pond seal nor the road.

**Clothes and the spear (W9-C).** Four garments — traveller's cloak, leather coat, hunter's mantle, ashen
shroud — each a curved sheet of cloth built in code and hung off the chest bone (`Wardrobe` finds the bone
by trying the usual rig spellings; failing all of them the cloth still hangs off the player). The owner's
rule was "press E and I wear it", so `Wardrobe` listens to `Inventory.item_added` and the newest garment
wins. A **spear** joins the weapons: longest reach, slowest swing, built in the hand from six primitives.
Five of them lie in houses, the leather coat in the house nearest the spawn.

**Stamina (W9-S).** `Stamina.unlimited`, switched on by `PlayerController.unlimited_stamina`: every spend
and drain succeeds and takes nothing. The bar stays, full. Nothing was deleted, and the jump probe switches
it back on so the cost-and-refusal rule is still measured on the real controller.

## Checks (console build, headless)

- Suite **PASS 419 -> 428, FAIL 0**, 0 SCRIPT ERROR in stdout and stderr, 51/51 files, per-file sum 428/0. Three new test files:
  `test_towers.gd` (12 tests), `test_garments.gd`, plus updates where the old intent was pinned.
- Live scene: **grass instances 12,142 -> 31,357** (blade verts 324k -> 718k), `grass_fill_worst_ms` **5.76**,
  p95 3.8, none over 8 ms; `PROBE sites fixed=3 spawned=91 props=34 pieces=709`;
  `PROBE fires total=107 drawing=13`.
- The denser field needed a bigger fill slice: `fill_budget_ms` **1.0 -> 2.2**. At 1.0 a sprinting player
  outran the fill and left cells empty inside 50 m; this is the honest price of the grass the owner asked for.

## What the tests caught that nobody would have seen

- **The first cut of the towers roofed every staircase at 1.2 m.** A floor slab spanned the whole storey, so
  the climb would have jammed halfway up. `test_towers.gd` walks the real treads and asks what is above each
  one; it failed on seven treads of the lighthouse.
- **The second cut left the top flight of every watchtower climbing to no floor at all** — the last storey
  built no ceiling, so the player would have walked up and fallen through.
- **The islet's first position put the light tower on the old road** and its skirt against the pond seal's
  apron. Both are rules the site tests already held for every other building.

## Not verified, plainly

- **Everything above is again unseen.** The green is a guess at a colour, the fence heights are a guess at a
  scale, no one has climbed a tower or worn a cloak. The one thing that is now certain is that the previous
  guesses were wrong, which is worth more than another round of confident numbers: **another recording,
  after this build, is the next most useful thing the owner can send.**
- **The cloak hangs rigid.** It is parented to the chest bone, so it swings with the body but does not
  billow, and it will clip the legs at a sprint. If it faces the wrong way, `yaw_deg` in
  `data/garments.json` turns it round.
- **The towers are empty.** Stairs and floors and a fire on top, nothing to find inside yet (W9-T2).
- **The spear does nothing special.** It is in the weapon table with the longest reach, and `Melee` already
  uses reach, but nobody has swung it at anything.
