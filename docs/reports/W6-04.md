# W6-04: grass frame cost and terrain build time, measured

All figures are **CPU-side, headless (null renderer), on the lead's machine**, from the new `PROBE cost` line in the vale
probe. Headless says nothing about draw calls, fill rate or GPU time, so "does it hitch?" is only half answered. The worktree
has no `assets/`, so the `ferns` and `sprigs` model layers did not build (3 of 5 layers, ~14 of ~216 candidates per cell
missing); a full cell costs a little more. UNVERIFIED by how much.

## Measured (3 runs each, before any behaviour change)
- **Grass, fixed 3 cells/frame:** 37 working frames filled 109 cells; **mean 3.5-4.0 ms per working frame, worst 5.4 ms** in
  three of four runs and one **10.3 ms** outlier (which frame: UNVERIFIED, probably the first). About **1.2 ms per cell**.
  Sprinting does not raise the per-frame cost (the cap is per frame either way), only how often frames work, so the worst
  sprint frame is the worst three-cell frame: over the 4 ms line.
- **Terrain:** wall `build_ms` 2082-2182 ms unthrottled, **4058 ms with `--max-fps 60`**, while CPU inside `_build_chunk` is
  **1.86-1.93 s**: height sampling **~1.05 s (55 %)**, LOD meshes **~0.8 s (43 %)**, collision faces **~43 ms (2 %, 15 chunks)**.
  `build_frames=234` for ~234 queued chunks: **a chunk costs ~7.4 ms, above the 6 ms `build_budget_ms`, so every frame builds
  exactly one chunk** and the build is frame-bound, not CPU-bound. The owner's 5150 ms fits ~234 frames at a lower frame
  rate or a slower CPU; UNVERIFIED which.

## What changed
- `grass.gd`: fill timing counters (two clock reads, three integer adds on a working frame, nothing idle); `fill_budget_ms`
  (2.0) exported, the fill stops after the cell that crosses it, `fill_cells_per_frame` stays the hard cap (Terrain's
  pattern). Test seams: `rebuild(data)`, `bind_heightfield(hf)`, `step(p)`, `positions()`. **After: worst 3.6-4.4 ms, mean
  2.6-2.8 ms, 50-51 working frames**; the `PROBE grass` line is identical (`cells=109 instances=8240 blade_verts=218944
  underwater=0 steep=0 floating=0 pending=0`). No density, spacing, colour or placement change; `vale.tscn` untouched.
- `terrain.gd`: `sample_ms`, `mesh_ms`, `collision_ms`, `build_cpu_ms`, `build_frames` in `stats()`. Measured only, not optimised.
- `vale.gd`: `PROBE cost ...` line. Tests: `tests/test_grass_budget.gd` (4: counters zero and monotonic; budget ends in the
  same lattice as the fixed count; budget respected and cap backstops it; a crossing under budget ends where a fresh lattice
  would) plus one live `PROBE cost` test each in `test_ground_cover.gd` and `test_terrain.gd`. No new child runs.

## Checks
- `--headless --import`: exit 0, 0 ERROR/WARNING lines, before and after.
- `run_tests.gd`: **PASS 282 / FAIL 16 before, PASS 288 / FAIL 16 after** (+6 = the new tests). The 16 failure lines are
  identical before and after and all missing-asset (`wick.glb`, prop `.gltf`, the two model grass layers); the
  `31 ObjectDB instances leaked` warning is identical too.

## Proposed, not implemented
- The 6 ms terrain budget buys nothing while a chunk costs 7.4 ms. Either raise `build_budget_ms` to ~2x a chunk during load
  (two chunks a frame, about half the wall-clock, ~15 ms frames while the valley is silent anyway), or run
  `TerrainMesh.sample_grid` (55 %, pure, read-only Heightfield) on a `WorkerThreadPool` task so the main thread only builds
  meshes (~3.2 ms per chunk). FastNoiseLite under concurrent reads: UNVERIFIED.
