## Animator (`player_animator.gd` 243 lines + `player_anim_rig.gd` 186)
`PlayerAnimator extends Node` (`%Animator`) builds an `AnimationTree` in code beside the glb's AnimationPlayer. Graph low→high:
BlendSpace1D(idle 0/walk 1/run 2) → TimeScale → Hit OneShot → Attack OneShot (clip via Transition) → Roll OneShot → Death Blend2
(held, re-seeked; a one-shot would stand the corpse up). API: `state_name()`, `is_busy()`, `current_state()`, `is_rigged()`,
`missing()`, `has_clip()`, `clip_count()`, `blend_value()`, `time_scale()`, `speed()`. Statics: `PlayerAnimator.locomotion_blend(speed,
walk, sprint)` (0→1→2, clamped, NaN/negative→0), `PlayerAnimator.resolve(dead, rolling, attacking, heavy, hit, invulnerable)`
(DEATH > ROLL > ATTACK > HIT > LOCOMOTION; hit hidden while invulnerable/rolling; a hit never interrupts a swing because Melee
never cancels one); `PlayerAnimRig.playback_scale/fit_scale/swing_duration/missing_clips`. `dodged`→Roll fitted to 0.45 s,
`swing_started(heavy)`→attack fitted to windup+active+recovery, `damaged`→HitRecieve, `Game.player_died/respawned`→death/clear.
Clips: Idle, Walk, Run, Roll, Sword_Slash (light **and** heavy — one sword clip exists; heavy = same clip over 0.98 s), Punch_Right /
Kick_Right unarmed, HitRecieve, Death; none missing. Gait clips looped at runtime (the `.import` that could is git-ignored, D-005).
**Foot sliding**: measured stance speed of `Foot.L` — walk clip 0.99 m/s, run 1.67 m/s at scale — vs game 4.2/7.0 m/s: planting
needs ×4.2; clamped to ×3.0 (`max_time_scale` export) ⇒ ~30 % residual slide at walk and sprint. Fix is tuning speeds or the clamp.

## Lead review (2026-10-25)
Verified independently: 228/228 tests after merging W3-04 alongside, zero import/parse/shader errors, the
animator probe reporting `clips=24 missing=none` with every required state resolved, and the eleven greybox
lines plus `--probe-bite` unchanged.
Three things this report did better than asked. It **measured the model's facing from the walk clip** (which
foot swings toward +Z and which drags back) instead of eyeballing a rest pose — the correct way to catch the
classic backwards-model error. It **measured foot-plant speed numerically** rather than calling the slide
"probably fine". And it **corrected one of my own decisions**: D-003 recorded that no CC0 deer existed, which
was wrong — `T6Cs7tmMHJ` is CC0 on Poly Pizza, so the shrunken-horse stand-in is gone and a real deer is in.
**Foot sliding, halved by the lead.** The walk clip plants 0.99 m/s and the run clip 1.67 m/s at this model's
scale, against a game walk of 4.2 and sprint of 7.0 — exact planting needs ~4.2x playback, which reads as
frantic legs. `max_time_scale` raised 3.0 → 3.5, cutting residual slide from ~30% to ~17% without that.
The other lever is lowering `walk_speed`/`sprint_speed` by ~15% on `PlayerController`, which removes the slide
entirely and would also make the 1 km valley feel larger — but that is a feel decision and the owner's to make.
**Also fixed:** `scenes/levels/treetest.tscn` and `scenes/environment/tree_test.tscn` were committed with a
UTF-8 BOM, which makes Godot's scene parser fail with `Expected '['` on a cold import. Pre-existing, found by
this agent, stripped by the lead.
Accepted as honest and open: nothing visual is verified; the roll plays at ~3.7x to fit a 0.45 s dodge and will
likely look frantic (a longer `dodge_duration` is the fix); the heavy attack is the light clip slowed because
the set has one sword clip; hammer and torch have no in-hand mesh; and the AnimationTree is built in code so it
cannot be inspected in the editor.
