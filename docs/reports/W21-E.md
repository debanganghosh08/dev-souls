# W21-E: terrain stripes and the flat green — tile breaking, macro mottle, dry/lush grass tint
No git, no editor, no runs (per brief). Files: `scripts/systems/terrain.gdshader`, `scripts/systems/terrain_material.gd`,
**new** `tests/test_w21_terrain.gd`. `tests/test_terrain.gd` and `data/areas/vale.json` untouched (the latter is another task's).
**Technique.** The stripes are one 2 m planar tile repeated 512 times across the vale: every directional feature lines up with its
copies along world X and Z, and 16x anisotropy (W21-B) only sharpens that. Chosen: Quilez's "texture repetition" blend plus
**rotation** — a value noise at `tile_break_metres` picks one of eight copies of the planar UV per region, each rotated by k golden
angles (none axis-aligned), rescaled ±10 % and offset; the two neighbouring copies are mixed across the region edge, the mix nudged
by the albedo difference so the seam hides in the darker copy. Normal and roughness reuse the albedo's weight, and the normal read
at a rotated UV is rotated back (`unrotate`) so bumps still light from the sun's side. Rotation is what kills stripes; an offset
alone shifts the same stripes. Rejected: stochastic triangle-grid texturing (always 3 samples per map, 3x fetches); a two-scale
blend (hides the period, does not remove it). The flat green: two octaves of value noise (`macro_metres` 61 m and 0.27x ≈ 16 m)
multiply the final albedo by 1 ± `macro_variation` on every layer, and the grass tint is swung between `grass_tint_dry` (warm,
yellower) and `grass_tint_lush` (cool, greener) by a 31 m noise, both near luminance 1.0 so hue moves and brightness stays the
macro's job. All noise is ALU (Hoskins sin-free hash); no new textures.
**Fetch cost per pixel.** Planar layers: 3 → **6** fetches per layer (albedo, normal, roughness, each twice). But mud and gravel
are now gated on weight (`if (w_mud > 0.001)`, coherent branches; before, all three planar layers were fetched everywhere and
multiplied by 0), so open flat grass is **9 → 6**; a grass/mud, grass/gravel or road band **9 → 12**; rock unchanged at 9 triplanar
plus whatever blends under it; worst case 18 → 27. `tile_break_metres` 0 = one sample per map. Four noise evals (16 hashes) added.

|---|---|---|
| planar mapping | one world-XZ tile, no rotation → 8 rotated/offset/rescaled copies blended by 9 m noise | `tile_break_metres` 9.0 (≤ 0 = off) |

| grass_tint, slopes 20/28/36, mud_height/blend, normal_strength 0.8, road, SPECULAR 0.3, ROUGHNESS 0.3–1.0 | unchanged | — |
Defaults are constants on `TerrainMaterial` (`TILE_BREAK_METRES` … `GRASS_TINT_LUSH`); `_colour` gained a `fallback` argument, white
by default, so `grass_tint` behaves exactly as before. Lead: add the keys above to `vale.json` → `material` to expose them; nothing
changes until a value differs. The vale's `grass_tint` [0.63, 0.82, 0.47] still sets the average hue.
