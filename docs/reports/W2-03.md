**Chunks/LOD/collision as implemented.** 16×16 chunks of 64 m, LOD0 2 m (33² verts, 2048 + 256 skirt tris). Each chunk
holds **4 MeshInstance3D** cut from one sampled grid (step 1/2/4/8) with `visibility_range` 0–128 / 128–288 / 288–576 /
576–∞, `FADE_SELF`, 12 m margins — chosen over rebuild-on-distance because 4.7 has no runtime LOD API and this costs no
script time per frame; the price is four resident meshes (~20 MB, estimated) and cracks hidden by 6 m skirts, not
stitched. Collision: `ConcavePolygonShape3D` from the LOD0 grid, layer 1, only for chunks within `collision_radius`+32 m
of the player, re-evaluated when the player changes chunk (polled every 0.4 s): 15 chunks at spawn. **Build:**
`begin_build(spawn)` sorts nearest-first, builds those 15 synchronously (tick 1 has ground), streams the rest at ≥1 chunk
per frame within 6 ms. Full build **2042–2171 ms** headless wall-clock (unthrottled, ~8 ms/chunk); at 60 fps expect ~4 s
for the other 241 — an estimate. **Bug the probe caught:** Godot front faces wind *clockwise*; CCW faces let the player fall to y=−154.
