**`Health`** (RefCounted, pure, no signals): `max_value` 100 · `current` · `invulnerable` · `damage(a) -> applied` (0 when invulnerable, dead or a <= 0;
clamps at 0) · `heal(a) -> restored` (capped at max) · `set_max(v, keep_ratio)` (refuses v <= 0) · `is_dead()` · `fraction()` (0 when max is 0) · `reset()` · `kill()`.
**`Damageable`** (CharacterBody3D): `@export max_health` 50 · `health` · `sync_health()` · `take_damage(amount, from) -> applied` · `is_dead()` · `health_fraction()` ·
signals `damaged(amount, from)`, `died(from)` · virtual `_on_died(from)`. The player does NOT extend it (no multiple inheritance; PlayerController already has
a base) but offers the same `take_damage(amount, from)` shape by convention.
**`Melee`** (Node3D, `%Melee` under the player): `try_attack(heavy) -> bool` · `can_attack()` (IDLE, PLAYING, not dodging) · `step(delta)` · `equip(id) -> bool`
(needs Inventory + weapon table) · `unequip()` · `equipped()` · `state_name()` IDLE/WINDUP/ACTIVE/RECOVERY · `damage_for(heavy)` · `stamina_cost(heavy)` ·
`current_damage()` · `effective_reach()` · `weapon_name()` · `apply_hits(candidates, pos, facing) -> hits` · `static in_arc(attacker_pos, facing, target_pos,
reach, arc_degrees)` (inclusive boundaries within 1e-4; inside the target = hit; zero facing = miss) · `_inject(game, inventory, stamina, body)` · signals
`swing_started(heavy)`, `hit_landed(target, amount)`, `swing_finished()`. Targets gathered once per swing on entering ACTIVE (sphere query, layers 4|5, hip
height); per-swing hit set; presses outside IDLE are dropped (no buffering). **`WeaponTable`** (static): `PATH`, `UNARMED`, `DEFAULTS`, `load_from(path, report_errors)`.
**`Creature`** (extends Damageable): `Disposition { PASSIVE, SKITTISH, HOSTILE }`, `State { IDLE, WANDER, FLEE, CHASE, ATTACK, RETURN_HOME, DEAD }` ·
`static decide(kind, dist_player, dist_home, sight, bite_range, leash, dead = false) -> State` ("within" is `<=`, "beyond leash" is `>`, dead wins) ·
`state_name()` · `is_dissolving()` · `motes_active()` · `home_position()` · exports below + `loot: Dictionary`, `death_flag`. Death: AI stops, collider off,
loot -> Inventory, flag -> Flags, body sinks 0.35 m and fades (`GeometryInstance3D.transparency`) while `Motes` (GPUParticles3D, 48 golden one-shot quads)
burst, `queue_free` after 1.2 s. Expects children `CollisionShape3D`, `Body` (+ children), `Motes`.
**`PlayerController` additions:** `@export max_health` 100, `fall_damage_per_metre` 12 · `take_damage(amount, from)` (0 while `is_invulnerable` or not
PLAYING; `Game.kill_player()` at 0) · `health_fraction()` · `fall_damage_for(drop)` · `get_stamina()` · `get_health()` · `get_melee()` · `_inject(game,
inventory = null)` · signals `health_changed(fraction)`, `damaged(amount, from)`. Auto-equips sword > hammer > torch on `Inventory.item_added`; respawn refills.
