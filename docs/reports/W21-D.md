**Handed-over foliage fix: half right.** `alpha_to_coverage` in render_mode: correct (MSAA 2x is on). `ALPHA_ANTIALIASING_EDGE = scissor`: not — with a
scissor set the engine centres the feather at `scissor + edge` (why StandardMaterial3D's default edge is 0), so 0.5 + 0.5 puts it at alpha 1.0 and leaves
crowns and patches at half coverage. Applied: uniform `aa_edge` 0.05 in both, plus `ALPHA_TEXTURE_COORDINATE = UV * textureSize(alpha_tex)` on the twig
card so the mip-coverage boost works (distant needles stop thinning); not on the patch, whose alpha is a radial falloff with no mip. Side effect: an
alpha-antialiased material draws in the alpha pass (still depth-prepassed, shadows still scissored), so crowns lose SSAO if it is on. Cost UNVERIFIED.
**TAA verdict: keep it.** No motion vectors for TIME vertex motion, so a bent blade reprojects as static. Tip speed is `strength × speed × 0.54` =
0.15 m/s at the vale's 0.26/1.1: 2.5 mm a frame at 60 fps, 0.4 px at 5 m, sub-pixel past 3 m, and the neighbourhood clamp bounds what history can add,
so ghosting should be invisible; the visible risk is the 2 m re-thin pop. Wind left as is, capped by test. If the owner sees smear: lower `wind_speed`
first, FXAA + MSAA 4x after. `project.godot` untouched.
