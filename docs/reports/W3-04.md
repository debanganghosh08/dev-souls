# W3-04 — instanced grass, ground cover, softer sun, grounded props

**Branch** `task/W3-04`, one commit. Headless only: counts, maths and shader parsing are proven; nothing below is a claim about how it looks or costs on a GPU.

## Grass lattice (`scripts/systems/grass.gd` + `grass_lattice.gd`, `grass_layer.gd`, `grass_cell.gd`, `grass_mesh.gd`, `grass.gdshader`, `ground_patch.gdshader`, `data/grass/vale.json`)
- `Grass` (Node3D) covers `radius` 70 m around the player in 12 m cells (109 cells in the disc). Each cell owns one pooled `MultiMeshInstance3D` per layer (5 layers, `top_level`, instance slots preallocated to `max_per_cell`). On a cell crossing `GrassLattice.cells_needed` + `cell_diff` decide which cells leave (slot returned to a free list, `visible_instance_count = 0`) and which enter (queued nearest-first, filled 3 per frame). That diff is the only allocation and happens once per 12 m walked, never per frame.
- Filling a cell: `GrassCell.prepare` samples a 5x5 height grid (25 `height_at`), derives bilinear height/normal, a hollow factor (corners vs centre) and the trees within reach; each candidate then costs grid maths only, plus one exact `height_at` for the y of accepted instances and an exact `slope_at` only when the grid slope is within 12 deg of the limit. RNG seed = `hash(cell key, layer) ^ seed`, so a cell regrows identically on every visit and the probe is reproducible.
- Pure, scene-free, unit-tested (`GrassLattice`): `density_at(slope, height, water, max_slope)` (1 below half the slope limit, smoothstep to 0 at it, 0 under water, squared ramp over a 1 m shore band, NaN/0/negative -> 0), `site_factor` (0 on plateau tops, fading in over half the blend), `cell_of`, `cell_centre`, `lattice_offsets` (capped at 48 cells per side so a zero cell size cannot hang), `cells_needed`, `cell_diff`, `thin_factor`. `GroundPlacement.aligned_basis` is the shared partial normal alignment.
- Blades are geometry (D-002): a clump = 4 tapered strips x 3 segments (32 verts), vertex colour dark root -> straw tip, UV.y 0..1 as the bend mask. Per-instance custom data gives tint/dryness/phase. Shader: lean (not scale) downwind by `wind_strength * gust * mask^2`, phase from world position so gusts travel as bands; `cull_disabled`, no texture, Lambert, normal rebuilt in `fragment()` blended 80 % toward up so both faces light like the ground. Wind uniforms driven from the script/JSON.
- Distance handling: **both** per-ring density (`visible_instance_count` = placed x `thin_factor`, 100 % inside 45 % of the radius down to 25 % at the edge, re-evaluated when the player moves 2 m) **and** a shader fade that sinks blades into the ground over `fade` [52, 70] m. No `visibility_range` on grass cells; the lattice itself bounds them.
- Ground cover (same lattice, same pool): pebbles (procedural 20-vert lump, 1.4x in hollows), leaf drifts (textured quad, ragged scissor edge from the leaf texture's own brightness, clustered under Forest trees x hollows), `fern_02_b` and `shrub_03_a` meshes at 0.3-0.7 scale, ferns clustered under trees. All layers: shadows off, patch noise for bare patches.

## Sun and vale palette (`sky.tscn`, `daynight.gd`, `sky_profile.gd`, `data/daynight.json`)
- Why it read as a pasted disc: `ProceduralSkyMaterial` draws the sun as `LIGHT_COLOR * LIGHT_ENERGY` inside `sun_angle_max` (was **20 deg**, `sun_curve` 0.15), and our scene sun is deliberately weak (energy ~0.7), so the sky showed a 20-degree flat grey blob barely brighter than the horizon.
- Fix: the scene `Sun` is now `sky_mode = LIGHT_ONLY` and a new `SunDisc` light (`sky_mode = SKY_ONLY`, no shadows, `light_angular_distance` 0.8 deg) draws the disc, so disc brightness is decoupled from ground light. Per keyframe: `sun_disc_energy` (2.8 at noon, HDR above the 1.2 glow threshold so it blooms as glare; 1.3-1.8 at dawn/dusk), `sun_glow_deg` (9 at noon, 15-19 at dawn/dusk) and `sun_glow_curve` 0.32-0.45 (steep falloff, long faint tail). Fog hazes it further: halo x (1 + fog x 12), disc / (1 + fog x 8). Defaults keep wood/barrens working unchanged.
- Horizon glow: new keyframes 5.5 / 6.0 / 6.75 and 17.25 / 18.0 / 18.75 with a desaturated rose-grey `sky_horizon` against a still-dark `sky_top`, and `sky_curve` (now per keyframe) tightened to 0.10-0.13 so the band hugs the horizon. ProceduralSkyMaterial's band is all-round, not directional; the directional part comes from the wider dawn halo.
- Palette: slightly bluer grey-blue sky top, fog 0.034 at noon (was 0.030) and 0.046-0.050 around sunrise/sunset, sun energy 0.78 -> 0.74, midnight ambient 0.14 -> 0.12 with the horizon kept lighter than the ground so silhouettes read. All test constraints (vale between barrens and wood in fog) hold.

## Prop alignment (`level_dressing.gd`, `ground_placement.gd`, `data/dressing/vale.json`)

## Decisions beyond the spec
Pure maths lives in `GrassLattice` (not static on `Grass`) to keep `grass.gd` at 245 lines; `grass_layer.gd` / `grass_cell.gd` / `grass_mesh.gd` split the same way. Grass shadows off. Two shaders rather than one with a texture branch. `haze_widen`/`haze_dim` are exports on `DayNightCycle` rather than JSON. `level_dressing.gd` grew 261 -> 272 (already over 250; alignment maths went to `GroundPlacement` to keep that small). `test_ground_cover.gd` runs a second vale child process (~40 s added to the suite).

## Gaps (honest)
- **Frame cost is unmeasured.** ~220 k blade vertices at the spawn before thinning plus ~545 small draw calls; cell fills cost ~25 grid samples + ~80 exact samples each, 3 cells/frame on crossings. A hitch on fast sprints across cells is possible; `fill_cells_per_frame` is the knob.
- Not seen: wind amplitude/speed, blade colours, leaf-patch edge, pebble size, sun disc brightness and halo width, dawn band colour, prop tilt amount. All tuned blind; data-driven for the owner to adjust.
- Grass does not know about dressing props or the water disc edge (clumps can poke through a boulder base); no collision, no player-trample.
- Grass y comes from the analytic heightfield; LOD1+ terrain further out deviates by a few cm, hidden by the sink fade.
- Headless `floating=0` uses the heightfield, not the rendered mesh; the greybox probe was not baselined before the change, only asserted unchanged by its existing tests.
