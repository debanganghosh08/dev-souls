# W6-03: weapons in the player's hand (sword, procedural hammer and torch, torch light)

## What changed
- **New `scripts/systems/player_weapons.gd`** (`PlayerWeapons extends Node3D`, `%Weapons` in `player.tscn`). Visual only: no damage,
  stamina or reach numbers; those stay in `WeaponTable`/`Melee`. Shows exactly one prop or none: the glb's own `Sword` node for
  `sword` (found by `sword_path`; a missing node is a `push_warning`, not a crash, and empties the hand), a built **hammer** (shaft,
  box head, two bands, 138 vertices) and a built **torch** (shaft, wrap, charred rim, ember, 152 vertices). Both are parented to
  `Middle1_R` (the `BoneAttachment3D`) so they follow the hand through every clip; a missing hand parents them to `Weapons` with a warning.
- **Grip frame measured, not guessed.** A headless probe instanced `player.tscn`: `Middle1_R` carries a world scale of 93.2 (the glb's
  armature is x100, FBX2glTF centimetre export, times the Body's 0.932), the Sword's local transform is grip at origin / blade down
  local -Y / width across X, and in the Idle clip the blade points forward and slightly up. Props are authored in metres, scaled by
  `HAND_METRE` (0.01) and given the Sword's transform (`DEFAULT_GRIP` is that transform copied, for when the Sword node is gone), so
  they sit where the sword sits; the torch head goes down -Y like the blade, which in Idle is forward/up, not at the ground.
- **Torch light**: `OmniLight3D`, warm (1.0, 0.72, 0.42), energy 2.2, range 9 m (exports) for the enclosed, unlit 10 x 9 m crypt.
  It hangs under `Weapons` (unscaled), not under the x93 hand, and `_process` moves it onto the ember each frame while the torch is out
  (probe: 0-1 mm off the ember). Flicker: three sines at unrelated rates, +-12 % energy, +-6 % range. Hidden and not processing otherwise.
- **Source of truth**: `resolve(Melee.equipped(), inventory)` — the weapon that actually hits, unless it is no longer held, else
  `PlayerLoadout.best_weapon`. Re-evaluated on `Inventory.item_added/item_removed`, on a new `Melee.weapon_changed` signal (emitted
  by `equip`/`unequip` only on a real change, so the inventory panel's equips also update the hand), and once on ready.
- `player_animator.gd` no longer toggles the Sword (its `sword_mesh_path`/`_sword` removed): one owner for the hand, not two.
- `player.tscn`: `Weapons` node after `Melee`; new `Mat_Wood` and `Mat_Ember` (emissive) sub-resources; reuses `Mat_Iron`, `Mat_IronDark`,
  `Mat_LeatherDark` (torch wrap) and `Mat_Ash` (char). `load_steps` 16 -> 19.
- `tests/test_player_weapons.gd`: 11 tests, no autoloads, fakes injected via `_inject` (same idiom as Melee).
